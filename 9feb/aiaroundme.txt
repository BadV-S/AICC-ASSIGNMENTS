Assignment Name: AI Around Me

1. Google Search
Uses machine learning ranking algorithms to analyze billions of webpages and predict the most relevant results based on user intent, keywords, and past behavior.

2. YouTube Recommendations
Uses collaborative filtering and deep learning models to analyze watch history, likes, and user behavior to predict and recommend videos

3. Google Maps
Uses ML-based traffic prediction models trained on historical and real-time GPS data to estimate travel time and suggest optimal routes.

4. Instagram Feed
Uses engagement prediction models to rank posts based on probability of likes, comments, shares, and user interaction patterns.

5. Gmail Spam Filter
Uses Naïve Bayes / supervised classification models trained on labeled spam and non-spam emails to detect unwanted messages.

6. Netflix Recommendations
Uses matrix factorization and deep learning recommendation systems to suggest movies based on viewing history and similarity patterns.

7. Voice Assistants (Google Assistant / Siri)
Use speech recognition models (RNNs/Transformers) to convert speech to text and NLP models to understand user intent.

8. Online Shopping (Amazon / Flipkart)
Uses predictive recommendation systems and customer behavior analytics to suggest products based on browsing and purchase history.

9. Face Unlock in Smartphones
Uses convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for facial feature extraction and biometric authentication.

10. Autocorrect & Smart Reply
Uses language models (NLP-based sequence prediction models) to predict the next word or suggest quick replies based on context.
