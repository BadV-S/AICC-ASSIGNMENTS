Weak vs Strong Prompts
1. Resume Prompt
Weak:
“Make my resume”
Strong:
“Create a professional resume for a computer science student skilled in Python, ML, and cloud computing. Include projects, skills, and achievements.”

2. Business Idea
Weak:
“Give business idea”
Strong:
“Suggest a low-cost AI startup idea for Indian market with high scalability and monetization strategy.”

3. Study Plan
Weak:
“Make study plan”
Strong:
“Create a 30-day study plan to learn machine learning from beginner to intermediate with daily tasks and resources.”

Conclusion
Weak prompts → vague results
Strong prompts → clear, detailed, high-quality output
