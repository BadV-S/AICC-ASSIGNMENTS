Objective
Use K-Means clustering to group customers based on their shopping behavior in a mall dataset. This helps businesses understand different customer types and design targeted marketing strategies.

Customer Groups Identified
1. High Income – Low Spending Customers
These customers earn a lot but spend very little in the mall.
Businesses may target them with exclusive offers or loyalty programs to encourage spending.

2. Medium Income – Medium Spending Customers
These customers have average income and spending habits.
They represent the regular customer segment and maintain stable revenue.

3. High Income – High Spending Customers
These are premium customers who spend frequently.
They are the most valuable segment and should receive VIP benefits or personalized promotions.
