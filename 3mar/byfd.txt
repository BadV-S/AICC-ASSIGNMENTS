Features and Labels

Feature (Input Variable):

Study Hours (Number of hours a student studies)

Label (Output Variable):

Marks (%) obtained by the student

In Machine Learning terminology:

X (Feature) → Study Hours
Y (Label) → Marks

Relationship Prediction

From the dataset, we observe a positive relationship between study hours and marks.

As study hours increase, marks also increase.
This indicates a positive correlation.

This type of problem can be solved using Linear Regression, where the model learns the relationship between study hours and marks and predicts future marks based on study time.
