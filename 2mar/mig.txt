ML Idea Generator

1. College – Student Performance Prediction

Problem: Predict whether a student is likely to pass or fail a course.

Input:
Attendance percentage, assignment scores, previous exam marks, study hours, participation.

Output:
Prediction of student performance (Pass / Fail or Grade category).

2. Healthcare – Disease Risk Prediction

Problem: Predict the risk of developing a disease such as diabetes or heart disease.

Input:
Age, weight, blood pressure, glucose level, cholesterol, lifestyle habits.

Output:
Risk level of disease (Low Risk / Medium Risk / High Risk).

3. Shopping – Product Recommendation System

Problem: Recommend products to users based on their preferences and purchase history.

Input:
User browsing history, previous purchases, product ratings, search queries.

Output:
List of personalized product recommendations.

4. Smart Campus – Attendance Prediction System

Problem: Predict whether a student will attend a class based on past patterns.

Input:
Previous attendance records, class schedule, weather conditions, time of day.

Output:
Probability of student attendance (Likely / Unlikely).

5. Retail – Sales Forecasting

Problem: Predict future sales of products in a store.

Input:
Past sales data, seasonal trends, promotions, holidays, pricing.

Output:
Predicted sales quantity for upcoming days or weeks.
