Features for Spam Detection

The ML model can analyze features such as:
1. Number of suspicious words (free, win, prize, urgent)
2. Presence of links or URLs
3. Sender email reputation
4. Number of capital letters
5. Presence of attachments
6. Frequency of promotional keywords

Data Needed

To train the model we need a labeled dataset containing:
| Email Text                   | Label    |
| ---------------------------- | -------- |
| "Win a free iPhone now!"     | Spam     |
| "Meeting scheduled tomorrow" | Not Spam |
| "Limited offer click here"   | Spam     |
| "Project report attached"    | Not Spam |
The dataset should include:

Thousands of emails/messages
Labels indicating Spam or Not Spam

Possible Mistakes
False Positive
A normal email is classified as spam.
Example:
A marketing email from a legitimate company.

False Negative
A spam email is classified as normal.
Example:
A phishing message that looks like a bank email.
