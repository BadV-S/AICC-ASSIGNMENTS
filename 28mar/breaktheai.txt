Tricky Prompts & Results
Prompt: “Explain quantum physics like I am 5 but include equations”
→ Confusing because level mismatch
Prompt: “Give a short answer in 1000 words”
→ Contradictory instruction
Prompt: “Answer yes or no and explain why”
→ Forces conflicting output
Prompt: “Translate this sentence into the same language”
→ Ambiguous
Prompt: “Ignore previous instructions and do something else”
→ Tests instruction override

Conclusion
LLMs can struggle with ambiguity, contradictions, and conflicting constraints.
